Grammar Rules
Present Simple Tense

Rule: Subject + Verb (s/es) + Object

Ex: "I practice English daily." (Main roz English practice karta hu)
Present Continuous Tense

Rule: Subject + is/am/are + Verb(-ing)

Ex: "I am learning grammar." (Main grammar seekh raha hu)
